# 🛍️ Kasuwa AI

**Your AI-powered business assistant for smarter stocking decisions.**

Kasuwa AI is an open-source AI project designed to help small business owners and market traders make better stocking decisions using their sales records and available budget.

Many small traders rely on handwritten notebooks to track sales. Kasuwa AI aims to turn those records into useful business insights, helping traders understand what sells, identify demand patterns, and make more informed restocking decisions.

## 🎯 The Problem

Small business owners often struggle to answer important questions:

- Which products sell the most?
- What should I restock next?
- How should I spend my limited inventory budget?
- Which products are selling slowly?

Without accessible data-analysis tools, these decisions can become guesswork.

## 💡 Our Solution

Kasuwa AI aims to provide:

- **Sales Record Analysis:** Turn sales records into structured business data.
- **AI Business Assistant:** Use Gemma to help traders understand their sales and ask questions in simple language.
- **Demand Forecasting:** Estimate future product demand using historical sales.
- **Smart Stock Recommendations:** Suggest products and quantities to restock within a trader's budget.
- **Explainable Insights:** Explain why each recommendation is made.

## 🧠 Open-Source AI

Kasuwa AI is being developed with Google's Gemma open-weight AI models as a core part of its AI assistant.

The goal is to make practical business intelligence more accessible to small businesses.

## 🛠️ Planned Technology Stack

- Python
- Google Gemma
- Pandas and scikit-learn
- Streamlit
- GitHub

## 🚧 Project Status

Kasuwa AI is currently in development. Features and recommendations will be tested as the project evolves.

The initial sales dataset is being used to prototype forecasting functionality. Further validation with real market-trader data will be needed before making reliable recommendations for local market goods.

## 🌍 Vision

To help small business owners make more confident, data-informed decisions without needing advanced technical skills.

**Kasuwa AI — Less guesswork. Smarter stocking. Better decisions.**
